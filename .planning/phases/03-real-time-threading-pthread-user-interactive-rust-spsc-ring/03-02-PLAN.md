This resolves the rtrb-vs-loom contradiction (D-R3): one ring, whose atomics and cells route through a `crate::loom` cfg-shim (D-R5), serves THREE roles with zero algorithm duplication — production (the `extern "C"` exports from Plan 01, now filled in), a 1M-frame std-atomic stress test, and a tiny exhaustive loom permutation test. Output: `src/spsc.rs` (the ring), `src/loom.rs` (the shim), the loom test, the 1M stress test, a criterion bench (with the optional `rtrb` cross-check), and the real `extern "C"` bodies wired to the ring.
