<objective>
Build the two-process proof harness (D-07) that proves IPC-02/03/07 end-to-end at the CORRECTNESS level: the daemon as producer, a `posix_spawn`'d consumer, the parent handing the child the rendezvous send right via the CF#3-verdict mechanism (02-SPIKES.md), then the full encrypted round trip — producer encrypts a FlatBuffers Sample, writes it into a ring slot, passes the shm fd via `FDChannel` (mach_msg + MACH_MSG_PORT_DESCRIPTOR), rings the doorbell and bumps `seq`; consumer maps the received fd, reads the slot, opens the AES-GCM box with the seq-derived nonce, decodes, verifies the Sample, and ack-bounces (D-02). The consumer obtains the key per the CF#1 verdict (Keychain access group OR over the mach_msg channel). An XCTest host (`HarnessE2ETests`) drives the round trip and asserts decoded == sent — CI-runnable on macos-15 M1 (correctness only; the sub-µs M4 timing claim is Plan 02-05).

Purpose: This is where the primitive becomes a working, verifiable transport. It wires Plan 02-02's Foundation-free transport and Plan 02-03's session crypto into a real producer/consumer, implements the CF#3 rendezvous, and rewires the Phase-1 daemon stub (which referenced the now-deleted ShmCheck) into the Phase-2 producer. It delivers the correctness half of SC#1 (round-trip succeeds) and the runtime proofs of SC#2 (fd passed, frame readable through it) and SC#3 (frame decrypts on the receiver).

Output: A producer (`Apps/CortexDaemon`), a consumer harness (`CortexIPCSession`), the CF#3 rendezvous C helper, and a CI-runnable end-to-end correctness test.
