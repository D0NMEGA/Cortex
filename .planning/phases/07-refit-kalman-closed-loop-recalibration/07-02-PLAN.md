<objective>
Implement the 6-DOF steady-state constant-gain Kalman step and the Gilja-2012 intent-rotation in the
`CortexReFIT` package, consuming the committed constants from Plan 01. The per-tick hot-path op is a
few fixed `simd` mat-vec products with zero allocation: predict → sync position externally → rotate
the measurement toward the target → constant-gain update → emit a refined 2-vector velocity. Extend
`hotpath-policy.sh` to cover the package (SC#3).

Purpose: This is the closed-loop algorithmic surface (REFIT-01/02). The rotation must act on the
MEASUREMENT before the update (D-05, not a cosmetic post-hoc nudge), align direction fully while
preserving decoded speed, and gate off inside the acquisition radius and when no target is active —
or the cursor "snaps" and the BPS is meaningless (RESEARCH §7 pitfall 4). Position is synced
externally because it is unobservable from a velocity measurement (RESEARCH §2.3).

Output: `IntentRotation.swift` + `KalmanFilter.swift` + their tests; `hotpath-policy.sh` extended;
CI runs the policy over CortexReFIT.
</objective>

boundary: the filter runs in the audio-callback regime (existing decoder pthread, SC#3), so any
Foundation/lock/allocation on the path is a correctness AND credibility defect. Input is the decoded

non-finite reject, but the filter must not itself produce NaN/Inf).
