`ndt1.data.load_session` that the fixture is built to pin.

Purpose: RD-02 claims the loader ingests real sessions correctly. Research proved it does not, in
three ways. (1) MATLAB writes empty cells as *truthy* HDF5 references to a `(2,) uint64` dataset

D-05 needs `finger_pos`, whose planar pair is rows 1-2, not rows 0-1 - taking 0-1 raises no exception
and silently halves R2 (C-02, P4).
