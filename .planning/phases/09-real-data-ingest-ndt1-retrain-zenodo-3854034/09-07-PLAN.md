<objective>
Fit the shipped model's velocity readout on real `finger_pos` kinematics and publish an honest
held-out R2.

Purpose: D-05 exists because without it the shipped `.mlpackage` would carry a real encoder and a
fabricated readout, and Phase 10's RD-07 could not fit `R` from real `z_decoded` vs `v_true`
residuals. The number this replaces, `velocity_r2.json`'s 0.99985, is a self-consistency artifact:
`test_convert_velocity_output.py:55` generates labels as `last_bin @ w_true + 0.01 * noise` and then
regresses those same rates onto them. D-10 replaces it with a held-out R2 against a constant
TRAIN-mean null, reported per axis, per session and pooled. D-08 publishes the whole lag curve rather
than the argmax, and D-09 keeps the fit closed-form so the 226/226 ANE-eligibility result is not put
at risk.

Output: `fit_velocity_real.py`, a `velocity` section in the metrics JSON, `09-velocity-evidence.md`,
and the `NDT1ANEWithVelocity` checkpoint Plan 09-08 converts.
</objective>

Readout geometry that fixes the lag convention: `VelocityHead.forward` reads `rates[..., -1:]`, the
LAST bin of the window, so "lag k" pairs the window ending at bin `i` with the velocity at bin `i + k`.

Primary-source lag anchor: `nlb_tools/make_tensors.py` sets `'lag': 140` ms for `mc_rtt` (7 bins at
20 ms); `mc_maze` 100 ms; `area2_bump` -20 ms. M1 leads hand velocity by 120-180 ms
(PNAS 10.1073/pnas.2212227120). A selected lag far outside 5-8 bins is a signal that the alignment or
the sign is wrong, not a discovery.

Expectation setting (09-RESEARCH section 7, assumption A2, confidence LOW): a rank-2 linear map from
96 context-smoothed rate values at a SINGLE bin should land well below the 0.633-0.838 R2 that
GRU/Transformer/RWKV/Mamba sequence models reach at 10 ms bins with nonlinear readouts
(arXiv 2406.06626). An R2 in the 0.1-0.4 range would be unremarkable for this architecture. Report
whatever appears (D-25).
