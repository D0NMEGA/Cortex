`gh api repos/D0NMEGA/Cortex/actions/runs` returns `total_count: 0` and `origin/main` is not a known

no-push option and the push-with-lint-soft-failed option. Plans 10-15 and 10-16 did the remediation;

bytes leave the machine they are public, and a later deletion does not un-publish them, so the pre-push
audit is a real gate and not a formality. `readme-policy.sh` already forbids PEM headers, inline
`MATCH_PASSWORD` assignments, emails and ASC issuer UUIDs in the README, and that forbidden set becomes
materially more important the moment the repo is pushed. And its outcome is not knowable in advance: a
hosted runner differs from this machine, so the honest plan reports what happened rather than what was
expected.
