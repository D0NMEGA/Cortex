<objective>
Surface the session's real `target_pos` through the loader (D-01) and turn one pinned `.mat` into a
compact binary export plus a JSON sidecar that Swift can read with no parsing risk (D-06, D-07).

Purpose: D-01 requires ReFIT's intent rotation to point at the target the animal was actually
reaching for, which means `load_session` must read a third behavior array. D-06 keeps binning in
`ndt1`, where `bin_spikes` and `bin_velocity` share bin-edge arithmetic by construction, because a
second binner in Swift is a silent drift hole. The one place a naive implementation goes wrong is the
target track: `target_pos` is a step function on a 15 mm discrete grid, and mean-aggregating across a
bin that spans a target change produces an off-grid target the animal never saw, which is exactly the
fabrication D-01 exists to prevent.

Output: `target_mm` on `load_session` and `SessionLoad`, `ndt1.replay_export` (writer + reader +
sidecar), `export_replay.py`, a committed synthetic fixture so CI covers the format with an empty
`Decoder/data/`, and a `.gitignore` entry for the real export.
</objective>
