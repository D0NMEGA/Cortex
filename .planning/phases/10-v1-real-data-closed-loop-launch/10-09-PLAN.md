Wire the structural gates the real-data numbers owe the repo: a provenance gate that ties every
published real-data figure back to the bytes it came from, and a schema test that pins the artifacts'
shape - both asserting nothing about the sign or magnitude of any result.

comparison rather than a token presence, with a self-test that mutates exactly one thing per case).
`test_metrics_schema.py` already carries the executable form of the no-measured-bar rule as a source
self-check. This plan applies both patterns to the two new artifacts and wires them, plus the Seam B
smoke, into CI.

three new CI steps.
