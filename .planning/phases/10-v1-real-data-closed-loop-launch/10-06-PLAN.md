`produce` builds `let pattern = patternF16(forSeq: seq)` per frame, encodes a FlatBuffers `Sample`,
AES-GCM-seals it with the seq-derived nonce (D-16), writes ciphertext||tag into the next ring slot with
a release store, rings the doorbell, then busy-polls the ack-bounce. The producer is Apps-target
orchestration where Foundation is allowed; the Foundation-free transport it calls
(`Packages/CortexIPC/Sources/CortexIPCTransport`) is hot-path policed and must not be touched.

`Apps/CortexDaemon/main.swift` is an argv-dispatched self-spawning harness:
`let mode = CommandLine.arguments.dropFirst().first ?? "produce"`, with cases `bench` and `consume`.
`Apps/CortexDaemon/Harness.swift` `runParent(frameCount:)` posix_spawns THIS binary with `consume` and
the child runs `HarnessConsumer.runChild`.
